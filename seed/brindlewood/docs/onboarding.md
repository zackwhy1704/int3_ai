title: New starter onboarding
scope: company-wide
owner: Grace H. (People)
source: Staff handbook
effective_date: 2025-09-15
---
Every new starter is assigned an onboarding buddy from their own team for the first six weeks. Laptops are issued on day one by the IT desk on the second floor.

New starters complete the health and safety induction in their first week. Warehouse staff must also complete forklift awareness training before entering the loading bay.
