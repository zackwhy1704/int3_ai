On 21 January the main conveyor in the warehouse stopped for three hours after a drive belt snapped. Around 340 orders missed the same-day cut-off.

Root cause was a belt past its replacement date. Actions: belts are now replaced every six months regardless of wear, and a spare belt is kept on site.
