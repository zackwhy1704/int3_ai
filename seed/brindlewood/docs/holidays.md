Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used by 31 March.

Leave requests longer than five consecutive days need two weeks' notice to your line manager. The warehouse runs a reduced rota between Christmas and New Year.
