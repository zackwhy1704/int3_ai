scope: company-wide
owner: Marcus O. (Finance)

Staff travelling for work should book economy class for flights under six hours. Hotel bookings are capped at 160 pounds per night outside London and 220 pounds per night in London.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip with itemised receipts. Meal allowances are 40 pounds per day when travelling overnight.
