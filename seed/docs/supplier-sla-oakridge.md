scope: operations
owner: Priya N. (Operations)
source: Operations drive
effective_date: 2025-12-01

Oakridge Timber, a fictional supplier, delivers pallets and crating timber to the Brindlewood warehouse. Standard orders are delivered within five business days of the purchase order.

If Oakridge misses the delivery window twice in one quarter, Brindlewood may apply a 3 percent service credit to the next invoice. Escalations go to the Oakridge account lead first, then to the Head of Operations.
