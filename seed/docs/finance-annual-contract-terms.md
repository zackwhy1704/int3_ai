Project Larkspur is the finance workstream for enterprise annual contracts. Its approvals are recorded here and are not yet published in the company-wide terms.

For enterprise customers on an annual contract, the refund window is extended to 45 days from the invoice date. This extension was approved by the finance committee on 18 March 2026 and applies only to annual contracts, not month-to-month enterprise accounts.

Refunds on annual contracts above 20,000 pounds require sign-off from the Finance Director before they are paid out.
