This document sets out the standard commercial terms for Brindlewood Supply Co. enterprise customers. It replaces all earlier versions of the enterprise terms.

Refunds. Enterprise customers may request a refund within 14 days of the invoice date. Requests received after that window are declined unless the goods were damaged in transit.

Refund requests must be submitted through the customer portal with the invoice number. The customer success team acknowledges each request within two business days.
