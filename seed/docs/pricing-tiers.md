Brindlewood Supply Co. sells on three tiers. Starter is for customers ordering under 500 units a month. Standard covers 500 to 5,000 units a month. Enterprise is for anything above 5,000 units a month and includes a named account manager.

Volume discounts on the Standard tier start at 5 percent for orders over 2,000 units. Enterprise pricing is quoted individually by the sales team.
